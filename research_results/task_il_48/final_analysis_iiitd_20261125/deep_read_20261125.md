# Deep review of available FedQCL training results

**Review date:** 2026-11-25 (Asia/Kolkata). This is a read-only analysis of result artifacts staged from MtechDEServer1, V100-server, and iiitd, plus the saved local `Trash/results_1` plot set. No dataset shards, environments, or model checkpoint tensors were analyzed. The 48-run campaign and the older 12-cell sweep are different experimental pipelines and are reported separately.

## Scope and evidence

- The authoritative Task-IL execution ledger has 86 jobs: 48 training, 19 data generation, and 19 validation. Its current `run_state` records all 86 as `VERIFIED_COMPLETE`; the 48 training rows are allocated Mtech 35, V100 7, iiitd 6.
- The final per-run verification receipt contains 48/48 `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` entries, source commit `38192b43b1f084333db415de388a240b8f3f35d1`, seed 42, and test evaluation. The analysis receipt has 48 completed, zero incomplete.
- The final-analysis manifest covers 299 artifacts; all 299 local files were checked against the SHA-256 manifest with zero mismatches. It includes the experiment result summaries, round/task/class/client diagnostics, verification receipts, and plots.
- Task-IL result CSV: [`experiment_results.csv`](analysis/experiment_results.csv). Narrative report: [`final_report.md`](final_report.md). Per-run verification: [`per_run_verification.jsonl`](verification/per_run_verification.jsonl).
- Ledger path assignments mean where each logical run was executed; runs migrated for throughput remain a single logical run. Four superseded attempts are retained separately (three checkpointed E08 migrations and a duplicate E06-03), not counted as additional experiments.

### Common Task-IL design and accounting

CIFAR-100, shared ResNet-18 backbone, task-specific heads, private replay, SGD, direct current-shard-weighted full-state FedAvg, fixed seed 42. All runs processed 450,000 current examples. Optimizer steps range 34,670–35,100 because task width and final-batch shapes differ; replay exposures range 225,550–445,900. The “communication” numbers are **logical serialized model/protocol bytes**, not measured network traffic. These are Task-IL results with known task identity/head, not Class-IL results. There is one seed, so numbers are descriptive and not statistical estimates. Across all 48 configurations the recorded logical communication sums to 1,127.07 decimal GB; they process 21.6 million example presentations and 1,677,670 optimizer steps (these totals count repeated exposures across separate experiments). Mean final macro accuracy is 76.34%; mean sample-micro accuracy is 76.33%.

## Family-by-family interpretation

- **E01 — participation and joint/disjoint curricula.** Within the joint curriculum, turning off 1/2 clients changed final accuracy 79.70% → 78.59% → 78.10%; turning off 3 ended at 79.84%, essentially equal to the full row in this seed, while using 59.9% fewer logical bytes (112.52 → 45.14 GB). In the disjoint curriculum the pattern is clearer: full 84.05%, then 83.05%, 82.08%, 76.83% as 1/2/3 clients are omitted; 3-off saves 59.4% bytes but costs 7.22 accuracy points. Keep joint and disjoint comparisons separate; they have different curricula and communication horizons.
- **E02 — sync frequency.** At fixed 450k examples and about 35k optimizer steps, 1, 2, and 10 syncs/task produce 78.83%, 80.71%, and 83.81%, while logical communication rises 4.72 → 9.21 → 45.14 GB. In this seed, reducing from 10 to 1 sync saves about 89.5% bytes and loses 4.98 accuracy points. Forgetting rises 4.47% → 6.11% → 7.89%, so final accuracy and forgetting move differently.
- **E03 — client population.** Population effects depend on sync count. At one sync/task, 2/3/10 clients score 77.68/81.75/71.84%; at five syncs/task they score 76.15/82.47/84.51%. Ten clients with one sync have especially weak current-task accuracy (55.50%) and low reported forgetting (0.34%), which is consistent with weak acquisition rather than evidence of unusually strong retention.
- **E04 — simultaneous task sharing.** Sharing a task concurrently with 2, 3, or 4 clients yields 84.51%, 83.12%, and 82.62%; bytes rise 24.93 → 27.18 → 29.42 GB. The accuracy decline across these three points is modest, but it is one seed and final current-task accuracy is nonmonotonic.
- **E05 — task width with partial participation.** Width 4 scores 77.24% at full participation and 60.00% with 3 offline; width 5 scores 72.68% and 49.72%, respectively. More offline clients reduce both communication and accuracy in both width slices. At width 5, 3-off costs 22.96 points and reduces bytes 58.5%. Width changes the task schedule too, so compare only within a width for participation effects.
- **E06 — cross-client recurrence.** Recurring the task on 2/3/4 clients gives 85.96%, 79.41%, 82.76%; not monotonic. Across class-level recurrence records, server accuracy gain is positive for 55–63% of recorded comparisons depending on recurrence size, with broad negative-to-positive ranges. Recurrence helps many classes but not uniformly.
- **E07 — communication-only outages.** Client 0 is intended to keep training while cut off from upload and download. Final accuracy for 1/2/3/5/10/45 outage rounds is 82.41/84.22/84.08/83.29/83.10/83.14%; there is no monotonic final-accuracy collapse in this run. Communication falls from the E01-05 reference 22.68 GB to 18.64 GB at 45 rounds (~17.8% fewer bytes). However, round-level diagnostics expose a rejoin shock: at the 45-round outage, server accuracy falls from 77.40% at round 45 to 75.65% after the rejoin round; for client 0/task 0, the recorded client accuracy is 96.5% before the rejoin download and 70.0% after it (model version advances to 47). The server later ends at 83.14%. This shows why terminal accuracy alone misses transient stale-state damage.
- **E08 — consecutive-task overlap.** Overlap settings 2/3/4 yield 54.48/57.15/45.88%, at roughly identical 36.66 GB. The highest-overlap case is worst here; the relation is not monotonic. In the class-context retention table, classes shared by adjacent tasks have a small positive mean change (~+0.6 to +0.7 points), while unshared classes average ~−0.9 to −1.4 points. This is descriptive class/task-context evidence, not a causal claim.
- **E09 — recurrence delay.** Delays 1/2/5/10/15 intervening rounds score 72.97/78.02/79.40/78.56/81.63%; forgetting falls from 17.18% at delay 1 to 9.15% at delay 15, though accuracy dips slightly at delay 10. All five use ~23.13 GB and the same nominal processed-sample and optimizer-step budget. In this seed, longer separation generally helps, but it is not a smooth law.
- **E10 — task width.** Width 3/5/10 gives 78.12/72.68/60.67%, while bytes fall 15.50/9.21/4.72 GB. Wider tasks reduce task count and reported forgetting (5.79/3.69/3.22%) but also reduce final accuracy. Width 3 has a final singleton remainder and a distinct micro-accuracy (77.68%); consult the chance-adjusted and micro fields for that edge case. E10-02 exactly repeats E05-05 by design and is not a second independent replicate.

## Older 12-configuration local-epoch/hidden-client sweep

Artifacts are under the staged `campaigns/cifar100_12_local_epoch_runs_20261122_012754/` trees from Mtech and iiitd. The 12 small HDF5 files are byte-identical across those host copies by per-file SHA-256; they are **12 configurations, not 24 runs**. The logs specify 5 clients, 5 sequential 20-class tasks, 50 global rounds, seed 42, ResNet-18, local epochs 1/2/10, LR 0.005, replay memory 500, replay batch 13, and random removal of 0/1/2/3 clients. Logs show Q-queue updates from `clientavg_tl.py`, so this is the older queue-regulated client path, not the conventional private-ER Task-IL implementation above.

At each server round, the old logger appends accuracy separately for each task seen so far. Fifty rounds yield 150 `rs_test_acc` values (10×1 + 10×2 + 10×3 + 10×4 + 10×5), while the log prints 50 means over the currently seen tasks. Therefore its `Best accuracy` is the **maximum of any one task accuracy at any time**, not the final average across all five tasks. ### Older-sweep interpretation and validity flags

- Across the saved metrics, increasing local epochs improves final five-task mean at every hidden-client setting: full 47.03→49.93→51.90%; 1 hidden 44.74→48.70→50.37%; 2 hidden 40.48→47.02→49.41%; 3 hidden 33.64→42.06→45.88%. This also increases local compute substantially (1→10 epochs), so it is not a communication-only comparison.
- At each epoch count, fewer participating clients generally have lower reported accuracy, but **do not use the full-visibility epoch-1/2 rows as valid baselines**: their logs contain CUDA OOM exceptions inside client `train` threads, yet the main process continued through evaluation and printed “All done.” This means the reported result may have silently omitted a client update. A recovery controller records a clean exit for a separate full-epoch-1 attempt, but no associated metric/HDF5 artifact is present in the staged result set; full-epoch-2 recovery attempts did not yield a verifiable result. Full-epoch-10 has no such OOM in its captured log.
- The 3-hidden epoch-1 original failed at round 0 with CUBLAS allocation errors; its clean retry is the retained result. The 3-hidden epoch-2 original finished the training/evaluation loop but failed creating the HDF5 due to a missing output path; its clean retry is the retained result. The original 2026-11-22 01:17 launch has only Python-2 syntax-error logs, no training.
- Because the two full baselines are suspect and every configuration has one seed, the older sweep supports a preliminary compute/participation trend only. It does not establish an unbiased effect size for hiding clients. The selected-client behavior also differs from the newer Task-IL outage experiment, where an offline client continues local training and only skips both directions of communication.

## Other result artifacts and limits

- The local `Trash/results_1` folder contains eight chart-only arms (joint/disjoint × Task-IL/Class-IL × FULL/OFFLINE; 128 PNGs plus README files). Those plots are an older, separate result set and lack machine-readable run configs/metrics, so they are not merged numerically into either server campaign. `Trash/results_2` is not present in the current live Trash directory.
- The staged server snapshots contain no executed selective-synchronization controller campaign. `run_all_sync.sh` artifacts are source/configuration and coordination evidence, not experimental outputs. Thus these results do not answer whether a small statistic can choose which client should sync.
- The result set is fixed-seed exploratory evidence. The Task-IL 48-run validator establishes artifact/completion correctness, not statistical significance, causal claims across confounded families, or physical-network bandwidth savings. Legacy full e1/e2 are specifically flagged as suspect.
